ESPFlight Hardware Reference v1.0 is an open hardware reference design for learning, building, experimenting with, and developing ESP-based drones.

The project provides a practical starting point that can be studied, built, modified, and adapted for independent projects and compatible hardware designs.

ESPFlight is an open platform, not a required official hardware kit. Builders may use the reference design as a foundation for their own boards, educational projects, prototypes, kits, and independently branded products.
